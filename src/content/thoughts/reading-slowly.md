---
title: Reading slowly
description: Why I started reading one book at a time again.
date: 2026-07-21
---

For a while I had six books open at once and finished none of them. So I tried something simple: one book, read slowly, with a pencil.

It worked better than I expected. I remember more, I underline less, and I've started writing small notes in the margins again.

*Slow is a feature, not a bug.*
