I wanted this site to feel less like a dashboard and more like a notebook. Warm paper, dark ink, and a single rose accent, like the red pen a teacher used to circle mistakes.

## The underline

Every link here gets a hand-drawn stroke instead of a straight line. It's a tiny SVG shape, tapered at both ends, that draws itself in from the left when you hover. It's the kind of detail nobody asks for, and the kind I like most.

## Type

Two typefaces carry the whole thing:

- **Josefin Sans** for titles, geometric and a little vintage.
- **Cormorant Infant** for reading, a serif with soft, calligraphic curves.

## Less, then less again

Every time I added something, I tried removing two things. The menu, the photo, the colors: all of it went through a few rounds of deletion before it felt right.
